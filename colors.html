Select a color and an informal type label to understand how the same coat can be presented differently.

Important distinction: American, European, and Russian are informal look or line labels used by breeders and buyers. They are not separate Maine Coon breeds, and appearance alone cannot prove ancestry. Tortoiseshell, torbie, and calico patterns are female-biased because black- and red-based pigment are X-linked; rare males are possible.
How to verify a color: Ask the breeder for photos in natural light, part the coat to inspect the undercoat, and ask how the color is recorded on the pedigree. Red and cream often show ghost tabby markings; smoke must show a pale base at the root; a photo cannot establish genetic color by itself.
Generated images are educational illustrations. Registry terminology can vary slightly; confirm color and health documentation with the breeder and registry.
