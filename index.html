How to use this guide: Start with the color guide if you are identifying a cat you already have, or with breeds and lines if you are trying to confirm whether a cat is a Maine Coon at all. If you are choosing a kitten, read the health page first, then take the worksheet with you when you contact breeders.
Educational reference only. Nothing here diagnoses a cat or replaces veterinary advice, and appearance alone never establishes pedigree. Any cat with breathing difficulty, collapse, sudden hind-leg weakness, severe pain, or marked lethargy needs urgent veterinary attention.
